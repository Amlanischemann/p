OUR FORMULATION APPROACH

Different biological pathways.
Clear scientific boundaries.

01

Circulation & nitric oxide

Citrulline can provide arginine for nitric-oxide synthesis. This biological pathway alone does not prove an improvement in erection hardness or duration.

02

Reproductive nutrition

Zinc contributes to normal fertility and reproduction; selenium contributes to normal spermatogenesis. Neither claim establishes treatment of infertility.

03

Men's healthy aging

Botanicals and antioxidants are positioned for general wellness. Clinical benefit for prostate enlargement or urinary symptoms has not been demonstrated for these formulas.

Sexual dysfunction, premature ejaculation and prostate disease require medical assessment. No product on this page is represented as a treatment for these conditions.
