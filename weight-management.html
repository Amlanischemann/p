A practical 28-day nutrition routine

01 · Breakfast

Choose a protein-containing breakfast; LIGHT DAY BREAKFAST can be one convenient option alongside other foods.

02 · Lunch

Build a main meal around vegetables, a protein source, whole grains or legumes and healthy fats.

03 · Optional format

Use a SLIM MEAL product as a meal replacement only after the final product is verified to meet the applicable composition requirements.

04 · Lifestyle

Include water, physical activity, adequate sleep and individual energy needs. Supplements are optional, not required together.

28 days describes a planning period, not a guaranteed weight-loss result. The system should be adapted to an individual's dietary requirements and medical context.

Product formulation and labelling

Where exact dosages are not given in the source product brief, ingredient fields on this page identify the proposed profile rather than a finished formulation. LIGHT DAY BREAKFAST uses the quantified recipe in PHARMED's original catalogue (P05). Supplement instructions, allergens, nutrient quantities and permitted health claims must be established from the finished manufacturer specification.

Glucomannan powder requires adequate water and appropriate choking warnings. This page does not make unverified weight-loss, fat-burning or carbohydrate-blocking efficacy claims.
