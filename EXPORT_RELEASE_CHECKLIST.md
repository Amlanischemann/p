# Export release checklist

1. Confirm exact destination country, importer status and supplement-vs-medicine classification. Claims about erection hardness, erectile dysfunction, delaying ejaculation, decreasing penile sensitivity, treatment of infertility, shrinking prostate, relief of BPH, healing or cancer prevention are not cleared for the supplement labels.
2. Confirm ingredient legality, botanical identity and extracts, concentration, solvent residue, contaminant limits, food/novel-food status and daily limits.
3. Contract GMP/HACCP-capable manufacturer; obtain specifications, batch CoA, identity/assay/impurity analysis, heavy metals, microbiology, pesticide testing and stability. Particularly screen sexual wellness products for undeclared sildenafil/tadalafil and analogues.
4. Test capsules/sachets for actual fill-weight, dose uniformity and stability; reconcile packaging count and doses.
5. Finalize market-specific label with ingredients/excipients/allergens, full nutrition quantities and NRV where required, directions, warnings, lot, expiry, responsible FBO/importer and mandatory language. Check local regulations, religious/halal claims and animal-derived excipients.
6. Reassess combinations and contraindications by a qualified regulatory/toxicology professional.
7. Confirm supplier certificates and product liability insurance, tariffs/HS classification, origin documentation, shipping temperatures, customs requirements, export certificates and distributor agreements.
8. Register/notify product as required before distributing. Avoid asserting Made in Germany unless supported by actual production/origin facts.
9. Finalize company email in assets/mens-health.js before publishing.
