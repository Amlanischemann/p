Reference directions & product information

Adults: 1 capsule daily with liquid; source suggests limited 4-6 week use.

Contains SOY and bovine gelatin. Under 18 excluded. Pregnant women, strong smokers and people with cardiovascular problems: source says medical advice. Avoid additional beta-carotene products. Biotin lab-test interference warning. No substitute for sunscreen.
