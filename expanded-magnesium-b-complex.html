Reference directions & product information

Adults: 1/2-1 tablet daily with a meal and plenty of liquid. Source distinguishes higher need from imbalance.

Not suitable for children/adolescents. Magnesium amounts above 250 mg may have a laxative effect in sensitive people. Do not exceed daily intake.
