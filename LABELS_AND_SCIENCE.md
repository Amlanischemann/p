## PHARMED NO FLOW | Nitric Oxide Pathway Nutrition
**Front label:** PHARMED NO FLOW | Nitric Oxide Pathway Nutrition | FOOD SUPPLEMENT | 30 sachets | 1 sachet daily

**Premium product-page description:** Targeted amino-acid nutrition with L-citrulline and essential micronutrients for adult men. A premium once-daily sachet concept for international wellness markets.

**Back-label panel, active composition per recommended daily serving:**

| Active | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| L-citrulline | 3,000 mg |
| Vitamin C | 80 mg |
| Zinc (elemental) | 10 mg |

**Suggested compliant nutrition claims (subject to market eligibility):** Zinc contributes to normal fertility and reproduction. Vitamin C contributes to the protection of cells from oxidative stress.

**Research and mechanism notes, not consumer efficacy promises:** L-citrulline can be converted to L-arginine, the substrate for nitric oxide synthase. Nitric oxide signals via soluble guanylate cyclase and cGMP in vascular smooth muscle. Direct clinical proof that this finished formulation improves penile hardness or erection duration is unavailable.

**Product-specific caution:** No immediate-onset, erection-hardness or ED-treatment claims. Review hypotension and medication interactions.

**Suggested compliant nutrition claims (subject to market eligibility):** Zinc contributes to normal fertility and reproduction. Vitamin B6 contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism.

**Back-label panel, active composition per recommended daily serving:**

| Active | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Magnesium (elemental) | 100 mg |
| Vitamin B6 | 1.4 mg |
| Zinc (elemental) | 10 mg |

**Suggested compliant nutrition claims (subject to market eligibility):** Magnesium contributes to normal muscle function. Vitamin B6 contributes to normal psychological function.

**Research and mechanism notes, not consumer efficacy promises:** Magnesium participates in normal neuromuscular function and B6 contributes to normal psychological function. **Suggested compliant nutrition claims (subject to market eligibility):** Selenium contributes to normal spermatogenesis. Zinc contributes to normal fertility and reproduction. Folate contributes to normal amino acid synthesis.

| Active | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Saw palmetto (Serenoa repens) fruit extract | 320 mg |
| Pumpkin seed oil | 500 mg |
| Zinc (elemental) | 10 mg |

**Suggested compliant nutrition claims (subject to market eligibility):** Zinc contributes to the maintenance of normal testosterone levels in the blood.

**Research and mechanism notes, not consumer efficacy promises:** Saw palmetto lipophilic constituents have been studied for interactions with androgen pathways and 5-alpha-reductase in experimental models; robust clinical reviews generally find little or no benefit of saw palmetto alone for BPH urinary symptoms. **Product-specific caution:** Saw palmetto extract identity and solvent matter; review novel-food, traditional-use, quality and local registration. No claims to shrink prostate, treat BPH or prevent cancer.
