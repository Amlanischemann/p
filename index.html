How the 28-day system works

01

Structure meals

Plan regular, enjoyable meals and an individualised moderate energy deficit where appropriate.

02

Choose complete nutrition

Use a meal replacement only where the final product meets full nutritional requirements. Otherwise enjoy a shake alongside a balanced meal plan.

03

Optional support

Consider fibre or other supplements individually, based on product specification and personal suitability. They do not replace meals.

04

Review at day 28

Assess consistency, hunger, energy and dietary quality. No fixed amount of weight loss is guaranteed.

What makes the system different?

Vanilla-cinnamon and chocolate-hazelnut taste profiles make convenient nutrition enjoyable. Combining structured meals with practical dietary routines may improve adherence, but there is no demonstrated additive fat-burning effect from taking all supplements together.

Product information

The PHARMED products shown are formulation and private-label concepts. SLIM MEAL is not represented as an EU-compliant meal replacement until its energy, essential fatty acids, protein quality, vitamin/mineral composition and applicable claim conditions are verified. Glucomannan products require precise intake instructions, abundant water and choking precautions. Ask a health professional when pregnant, taking medication or living with a relevant medical condition.
