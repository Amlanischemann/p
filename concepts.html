What counts as a German-market-compliant product?

Each product must pass food-versus-medicine classification, novel food checks where relevant, compositional and safety assessment, permitted sources, contaminant/stability testing, nutrition and allergen label verification and health-claim clearance. German food supplements require BVL notification under §5 NemV; this is not an approval. Protein powder and herbal teas may be ordinary foods instead.
